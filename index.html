Chuijia Wang

I am a postdoc at Department of Mathematics, The University of Hong Kong. Mentor: Kei Yuen Chan

I received my Ph.D. from National University of Singapore under the supervision of Wee Teck Gan.

Before joining HKU, I was a postdoctoral researcher at
Technion (2021.11-2023.12) Mentor: Maxim Gurevich;
the Chinese University of Hong Kong (2024.04-2025.12) Mentor: Jiu-Kang Yu.
During 2024.01-2024.03, I was an academic visitor at Kyoto University hosted by Masao Oi and Atsushi Ichino.

Research interests

representation theory of $p$-adic groups; relative Langlands programme: distinction and Langlands functoriality; Galois cohomology; intertwining operators; Bruhat--Tits theory; Kazhdan--Lusztig theory
