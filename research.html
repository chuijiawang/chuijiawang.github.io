Research

My research focuses on the representation theory of reductive groups and Lie theory, and their applications to number theory, combinatorics, and mathematical physics.

More precisely, I am broadly interested in representation theory of p-adic groups; relative Langlands programme: distinction and functoriality; Galois cohomology; intertwining operators; Bruhat–Tits theory and Kazhdan–Lusztig theory.
