Research

My research focuses on the representation theory of reductive groups and Lie theory, as well as their applications in other subjects such as number theory, combinatorics, and mathematical physics.

More precisely, I am broadly interested in the topics below.

Representation theory of p-adic groups
Relative Langlands programme: distinction and functoriality
Galois cohomology
Intertwining operators
Bruhat–Tits theory
Kazhdan–Lusztig theory
